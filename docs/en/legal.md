# Responsible use, privacy, and limits

OSINT Atlas catalogs sources and methods. OSINT means Open Source Intelligence. It does not collect personal case files and it does not grant rights over the pages it links to.

The same text in Spanish is [uso responsable](https://comunidad-de-inteligencia.github.io/osint-atlas/legal/).

## How to use it

- Have a legitimate, concrete, and proportionate purpose.
- Collect the minimum data and avoid a lasting profile of a person.
- Respect authentication, CAPTCHA, query limits, and the terms of the service. A CAPTCHA is the test a site uses to tell a person from a program.
- Do not add leaks, credentials, stolen material, or methods for bypassing controls.
- Check the license and the reuse terms on the final source.
- Separate facts, inferences, and suspicions.
- Remove copies of personal alerts when they are no longer in force.

## Sensitive material

Do not add sexual, violent, or abuse material to this repository, to an issue, or to a change proposal. For possible child sexual abuse material, keep only the minimum reference and use a competent reporting line. Do not download it, do not store it, and do not share it.

## Legal information

The documentation offers a general method. It is not legal advice. In Spain, the consolidated texts of the Boletín Oficial del Estado (BOE), the official state gazette, make consultation easier and are informative. For a legal purpose, consult the official publication.

## Training and retrieval with a model

A public source is not permission to turn it into a training corpus. Any use has to look at the license, personal data, purpose, how long it is kept, and what transformations are made. This project's index covers its own entries and guides.

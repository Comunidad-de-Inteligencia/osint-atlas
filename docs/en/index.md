# OSINT Atlas

A catalog you consult. OSINT means Open Source Intelligence: looking things up with information a person can obtain lawfully, without breaking into anyone else's system.

It helps you choose a source, understand what a result can say, and see what is still unchecked. It is not an application, not a viewer, and not a live map. It does not receive reports and it does not send alerts.

> If you are in immediate danger, contact the official emergency service where you are. The [emergency procedure](https://comunidad-de-inteligencia.github.io/osint-atlas/procedimientos/emergencia-inmediata/) and the [official assistance routes](https://comunidad-de-inteligencia.github.io/osint-atlas/contactos/) come before any technical entry. Those pages are in Spanish.

Sponsorship does not buy a catalog entry or a review.

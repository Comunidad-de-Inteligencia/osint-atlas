# OSINT Atlas

A reference catalog. It helps you choose an open source, read what a result can mean, and see what is still unchecked. It is not an application, a map viewer, or a live picture.

> If you are in immediate danger, contact the official emergency service where you are. Open the [emergency procedure](https://comunidad-de-inteligencia.github.io/osint-atlas/procedimientos/emergencia-inmediata/) and the [official routes](https://comunidad-de-inteligencia.github.io/osint-atlas/CONTACTOS/). Atlas does not receive reports and does not send alerts.

Sponsorship does not buy a catalog entry or a review.
